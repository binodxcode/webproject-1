Our Next Adventures

We are thrilled to announce our schedule for the upcoming season. Each trek is fully guided by experienced local Sherpas and includes all necessary permits and accommodations. We carefully plan each route to ensure maximum safety and cultural immersion. Booking spots are limited to maintain a low environmental impact and provide a better experience for every participant. Please review the itineraries below and use the contact page to reserve your place. Do not wait, as our most popular treks fill up quickly due to high demand and the quality of our service.

Current Available Treks

  1. Annapurna Base Camp (ABC): A challenging 12-day trek through diverse landscapes.
  2. Langtang Valley: A scenic 7-day trek known for its beautiful monasteries and wildlife.
  3. Mardi Himal Trek: A shorter, 5-day adventure with close-up views of Machhapuchhre.

What's Included in the Fee

  • Accommodation and meals during the trek.
  • Experienced, licensed guide and porter services.
  • Trekker Information Management System (TIMS) card and permits.
