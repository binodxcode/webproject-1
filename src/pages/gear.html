Packing Essentials for Your Trek

Proper gear is the difference between an enjoyable trek and a miserable one. Since weather conditions in the Himalayas can change rapidly, layering clothing is essential for temperature regulation. Always invest in high-quality, broken-in hiking boots to prevent blisters and ensure proper ankle support on uneven terrain. This checklist covers the basics needed for a standard 7- to 14-day high-altitude trek. Do not overpack; porters have weight limits, and you will be carrying a daypack yourself. We highly recommend waterproof outerwear and headlamps for early morning starts or emergencies.

Remember to consult our experts if you have any questions about specific brands or alternatives.

Mandatory Clothing Items

  • Waterproof and windproof shell jacket.
  • Thermal base layers (top and bottom).
  • High-quality wool or synthetic hiking socks (minimum 4 pairs).
  • Insulated down jacket for evenings and high altitude.

Essential Accessories

  1. Durable Hiking Boots (broken in).
  2. Trekking poles (highly recommended for descents).
  3. UV protection sunglasses and sunscreen.
  4. Headlamp with spare batteries.
